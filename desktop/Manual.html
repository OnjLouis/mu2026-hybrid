MU2000

Installation and ROMs

The Apple Silicon Mac installer includes the standalone app, Audio Unit and VST3. The Windows x64 installer includes the standalone app and VST3. This is not an update for the legacy Mu2026 Hybrid VST2.

No Yamaha firmware, waveform ROMs or expansion boards are supplied. Start the standalone app, open Preferences, and choose your own S-MU2000-compatible ROM folder under General. Restart the standalone and any plug-in hosts after changing this location.

Keyboard and MIDI

Press Command or Control plus comma for Preferences. Press F1 on an instrument control for its help. The standalone Musical Keyboard is available with Command or Control plus K; Z and X change octave, and C and V change velocity. Select Hide to Menu Bar on Mac (Command+Shift+H) or Hide to System Tray on Windows (Ctrl+H) to hide the window without stopping MIDI. Select Show Controls from the menu bar or system tray to return.

The Mac standalone offers four virtual MIDI ports. Windows uses system MIDI inputs. Plug-in MIDI from a host goes to port A. This release covers the base MU2000 firmware; VL/PVL, AN and other expansion engines are not included.

In Audio and MIDI, Windows offers WASAPI and ASIO. Choose ASIO, then your interface, sample rate and buffer. Play test phrase plays a short piano phrase through the selected output without changing the current sounds.

On Windows, Alt+W focuses Current sound and Alt+B focuses Bank MSB. In Preferences, Alt+G, Alt+A and Alt+P select General, Audio and MIDI, and Presets. Alt+D focuses the audio driver on the Audio and MIDI tab. Your screen reader can announce additional shortcuts as you move through the controls.

Presets

Open Preferences, then Presets, to choose destinations or generate Komplete Kontrol and native VST3 presets. Komplete Kontrol presets have audio previews and are listed under User, Yamaha, MU2000 after a User library rescan. VST3 presets are grouped into Programs and Kits by bank. Generation replaces only the application's own unchanged generated folder, and cancel leaves that folder intact.

Updates

Choose Check for Updates in Preferences or Help. If a newer MU2000 desktop version is available, choose Download and Open Installer. The app verifies the installer before opening it. Save your work, stop MIDI playback and unload MU2000 from audio hosts, then choose Install. The installer updates the standalone and plug-in formats together, without replacing ROMs, presets or preferences.

Project recall

Keep a separate project version before reassigning several older Komplete Kontrol instances. A single reassignment to the generated library has been verified in Logic, but one older project became silent after multiple reassignments. A pre-reassignment copy still plays.
